Type soul is a game based on "bleach". It features many races such as soul reaper, quincy, arrancars and fullbringers.

Soul reapers

✦

In Type Soul, Soul Reapers (Shinigami) are one of the primary playable races relying on spiritual pressure and swordplay Players begin as Academy Students and advance by refining their combat skills, flash steps, and Zanpakuto mastery. A Soul Reaper's power spikes significantly upon unlocking Shikai, achieved by meditating and defeating their inner spirit to gain elemental powers. True mastery culminates in unlocking Bankai, granting immense area-of-effect damage, distinctive weapon forms, and devastating ultimate abilities. Balancing high-speed melee strikes, Kido spells, and versatile skill trees, Soul Reapers excel as versatile, high-mobility combatants in both competitive PvP and faction battles.
